06 — THE FALLEN

Bring your fallen home.

Every dead coin in your wallet still holds about 0.002 SOL of rent in its token account. VICZ finds them, closes them, and that SOL comes back to you.

  1. Connect your wallet. VICZ reads your token accounts, nothing else.
  2. It lists every empty account and every leftover worth under $1.
  3. You pick, you sign. The accounts close and the rent comes straight back to you.
  • You sign every transaction in your own wallet. VICZ never holds or sees a key.
  • Nothing worth $1 or more, no NFT and no stablecoin is ever touched.
  • Dust is only burned when you pick it. Empty accounts are just closed.

07 — HQ

$VICZ

The first in-game trenches for memecoins. Built for the week Vice gets loud.

Everything in this world runs on live data: the soldiers, the billboards, the blimp, the radio and the coins floating in the streets. Press Play and drive it.

What is VICZ?

A 3D world built from the live pump.fun board. The hottest coins walk the trench as soldiers, new launches enlist in the barracks, and every graduation climbs out and charges the PumpSwap bunker.

Is the data real?

Yes. Coins and moves come from Jupiter's public data, launches and graduations from PumpPortal's live stream, and the whales from their public wallets on Solana. When a feed is down, the world says so instead of making numbers up.

What does bring your fallen home do?

It closes the empty token accounts your dead coins left in your wallet and returns the rent locked in each one, about 0.002 SOL. You see every account and the SOL before you sign. Nothing worth $1 or more is touched.

How do I play?

Hit Play on the first screen, or Drive in the menu. W A S D or the arrow keys drive, Shift boosts, Space drifts, T changes the time of day and Esc gets you out. On a phone you get a thumb stick and two buttons.

What are the coins floating in the streets?

The hottest coins on the line right now, in their real logos. Drive through one and its live move and market cap come up. Green beams are pumping, pink ones are dumping, and each one comes back a little later.

Does VICZ hold my keys?

Never. Your wallet builds nothing and VICZ sends nothing without your signature. The site makes unsigned transactions, your wallet shows and signs them.

Is this connected to GTA?

No. VICZ is an independent fan-made world. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Not financial advice. Memecoins are volatile and can go to zero.
