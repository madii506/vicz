07 — HQ

$VICZ

The first in-game trenches for memecoins. Built for the week Vice gets loud.

What is VICZ?

A 3D world built from the live pump.fun board. The hottest coins walk the trench as soldiers, new launches enlist in the barracks, and every graduation climbs out and charges the PumpSwap bunker.

Is the data real?

Yes. Coins and moves come from Jupiter's public data, launches and graduations from PumpPortal's live stream, and the whales from their public wallets on Solana. When a feed is down, the world says so instead of making numbers up.

What does bring your fallen home do?

It closes the empty token accounts your dead coins left in your wallet and returns the rent locked in each one, about 0.002 SOL. You see every account and the SOL before you sign. Nothing worth $1 or more is touched.

Does VICZ hold my keys?

Never. Your wallet builds nothing and VICZ sends nothing without your signature. The site makes unsigned transactions, your wallet shows and signs them.

Is this connected to GTA?

No. VICZ is an independent fan-made world. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Not financial advice. Memecoins are volatile and can go to zero.
